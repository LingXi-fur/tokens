# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Protect other people's privacy and never post their logs, paths, sessions, prompts, or credentials.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sustained bad-faith interaction is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
